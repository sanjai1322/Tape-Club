## What you must keep when sharing or forking

- **Music:** keep the credit "Music by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under CC BY 4.0" with a link to the license. The site already shows this in the footer while a song plays, in the Record Store, and on `music-credits.html`.
- **Fonts:** keep `FONT-LICENSES.txt` next to the font files. Fonts can't be sold on their own.
- **Three.js and GSAP:** keep their license files and the headers inside the files.
- **Artwork:** replace the cassette art and icons with your own before publishing a fork.

## Songs listeners add themselves

YouTube songs play through YouTube's official embedded player under
[YouTube's Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms); no audio is copied
or stored. Files listeners upload stay in their own browser and are never sent to
a server. Listeners are responsible for having the right to play what they add.
